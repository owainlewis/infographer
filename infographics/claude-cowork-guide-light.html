How To Master Claude Cowork

Everything you need to understand as a beginner in 30 seconds.

What Is Claude Cowork?

An AI assistant that lives on your computer and does things for you. You describe the task, it executes, you approve.

  • Reads your files and controls your browser
  • Opens Gmail, Google Sheets, Slack, Notion
  • Fills forms, sends emails, organises folders
Cowork vs Code
Cowork
  • For founders and non-technical people
  • Visual interface, no terminal needed
  • Point-and-click with approval steps
  • File management, browser tasks, workflows
Code
  • For engineers and developers
  • Lives in your terminal and codebase
  • Full customisation with hooks and CI
  • Building software, scripts, codebases

Workflow In Practice

Example: receipts to expense report in 3 minutes.

Step 1
Mount your receipts folder

Grant Cowork access. It scans every PDF and image inside the folder.

Step 2
Renames and organises each file

Extracts dates from receipts and renames files. Asks if anything's unclear.

Step 3
Builds a spreadsheet from the data

Pulls merchant, date, amount, category. Creates a formatted report in Sheets.

Step 4
Drafts and sends the email

Opens Gmail, attaches the sheet, writes the message. You review and send.

Getting Started
1Download the Claude desktop app from claude.ai
2Install the Chrome extension for browser control
3Mount the folders you want Cowork to access
Pro Tip

Don't over-customise at the start. Get value from vanilla Cowork first, then add Skills and Plugins when you spot friction in specific tasks.
