System Design Patterns

Nine essential patterns every engineer should know.

Overview

Design patterns are reusable solutions to common problems in software architecture. They provide a shared vocabulary for teams and help avoid reinventing the wheel. Understanding these patterns is essential for building scalable, resilient systems.

Retry

Automatically re-attempt failed operations with exponential backoff and jitter.

Resilience
Event-Driven Architecture

Decouple services using asynchronous events. Producers emit events without knowing who consumes them. Enables independent scaling and fault isolation across service boundaries.

Kafka RabbitMQ EventBridge Pub/Sub Async
Key Principles
  • Loose coupling between services
  • Single responsibility per service
  • Design for failure and recovery
  • Prefer async over sync calls
  • Use idempotent operations
  • Monitor at every boundary
  • Automate everything
