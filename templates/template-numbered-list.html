7 Habits of Highly
Effective Engineers

1
They write things down

Document decisions, not just code. The best engineers leave a trail of context.

2
They say no more than yes

Protecting focus is a skill. Every yes to one thing is a no to everything else.

3
They read more code than they write

Understanding existing systems matters more than adding new ones. Read the PR before you comment.

4
They automate the boring stuff

If you do it twice, script it. Time invested in automation compounds.

5
They ask obvious questions

The "dumb" question in the room is usually the most important one. Senior engineers ask them fearlessly.

6
They ship small and often

Small PRs get reviewed faster, merged sooner, and break less. Batch size is the hidden variable.

7
They take breaks seriously

Burnout doesn't announce itself. The best engineers protect their recovery time.
