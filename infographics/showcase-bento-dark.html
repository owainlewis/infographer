The gap between AI research and production is where most teams fail. A solid toolkit turns experiments into reliable systems. The right abstractions let you swap models without rewriting code, observe behaviour in production, and iterate fast.

Core Principles

Model-agnostic abstractions — never couple business logic to one provider

Structured outputs — parse every LLM response into typed schemas

Eval-driven development — measure before you ship, measure after

Graceful degradation — fallback chains, not single points of failure

2. Evaluate — build your test suite

3. Harden — add retries and fallbacks

4. Observe — traces and cost tracking
