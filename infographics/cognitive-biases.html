10 Cognitive Biases

01 Overconfidence

Believing you know more than you do.

Fix: Start with a pilot before going all in.

02 Confirmation Bias

Only seeing what supports your beliefs.

Fix: Appoint a devil's advocate in key meetings.

03 Groupthink

Avoiding conflict to keep the peace.

Fix: Use anonymous input to surface real opinions.

04 Authority Bias

Overweighting opinions from "experts."

Fix: Require evidence, no matter who's speaking.

05 Anchoring Bias

Fixating on the first number you hear.

Fix: Compare at least three independent options.

06 Availability Bias

Overreacting to what's memorable.

Fix: Focus on trends, not outliers.

07 Recency Bias

Overvaluing the latest data.

Fix: Use rolling averages to spot real patterns.

08 Status Quo Bias

Favoring the familiar over the better.

Fix: Ask "would we choose this today?"

09 Sunk Cost Fallacy

Throwing good money after bad.

Fix: Define a kill switch in advance.

10 Survivorship Bias

Only studying the winners.

Fix: Study failures too before deciding.
